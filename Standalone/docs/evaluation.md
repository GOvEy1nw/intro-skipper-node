# Evaluation inputs

Keep labels private and do not commit media files or personal paths. The template uses canonical decimal tick strings and labels programme intervals separately from proposed skip segments. `category` and `split` are reporting fields; `split` should identify held-out data before a profile is frozen.

Use `evaluate --results results.json --labels labels.json --out score.json` and `compare --baseline baseline.json --candidate results.json --labels labels.json --out comparison.json`. Labels may bind a media source key and file-version token, and credit boundaries may carry minimum/maximum tick bands. Report erroneous programme duration, false positives, missed credit duration/blocks, boundary error, review rate, coverage, and hardware/dependency context. A representative held-out corpus is pending; this document makes no production accuracy claim.

Human timings supplied to whole seconds should retain that precision in their private source reference and `category`; do not imply frame-accurate labels. Data already inspected or used to tune a rule belongs in `split: "Calibration"`, rather than a held-out split. The normal evaluator counts accepted skips only. Experimental proposals need a separately identified hypothetical-skip comparison; scoring them does not approve a detector or accept its proposals.

The experimental credits policy applies the user-reported group rules in order to coarse samples, normally two seconds apart:

1. Keep consecutive frames with pooled byte entropy at most 0.2 or at least nine positive text cells. Other frames close the group and remain available for gap measurements.
2. Discard a group containing five consecutive frames with entropy at least 0.7, positive text count and coordinates, counts within two and each coordinate within 0.01 of the run's first frame. Every possible five-frame starting point is checked. Before joining, also discard raw groups of at most four frames when every frame has entropy at least 0.7 and fails the existing text-layout label. This additional scanner safeguard prevents intermittent scene signs from chaining into real credits; it is not a reported Plex rule. Wide credit layouts and low-entropy cards remain eligible.
3. Join neighbouring groups when their last-to-first sample-index difference is at most four. At exactly five, join only when both complete group entropy means are strictly below 0.6 and the directed entropy drop from the earlier group's last frame to the later group's first is below 0.7.
4. Discard groups with at most five kept frames. Valid one-based frame indexes cannot be greater than the window's sample count, so the reported beyond-count exception cannot occur without synthetic frames.
5. Join timestamp gaps of at most ten seconds, including short story scenes. Measure longer gaps as described below. Include a flat final tail, or treat a group ending on either of the last two samples as reaching EOF.
6. Discard groups with at most ten kept frames unless they end within the last three samples.
7. Consider position only for frames with at least nine positive cells. A frame passes with mean cell coordinates strictly inside `0.10 < x < 0.90` and `0.10 < y < 0.70`; entropy at least 0.75 tightens the upper Y bound to 0.65. Discard the group if fewer than 60% pass. Exactly 60% passes; no eligible frames means no position rejection.
8. Discard a group with at most 30 kept frames if the next group starts at least 100 sample indexes after its end. The last group is exempt.
9. Count opening frames with more than nine text cells and entropy above 0.5. If there are more than four, retain the group's first frame as its start; otherwise skip that opening and choose the next frame with any text. Discard a start that is not before the end.

Index joins precede timestamp joins. Aggregate group tests use kept members; joining does not add excluded gap frames to their denominators. Flat tail absorption adds its actual frames, while the final-two-sample EOF exception leaves the raw endpoint on the group's last kept frame. Missing features or decode-cadence gaps cannot support joining or tail extension. Each suppression is recorded as an analysis warning. The latest reported flow explicitly makes exactly ten seconds inclusive, superseding the earlier strict-under-ten rule.

Longer timestamp gaps use complete coarse samples strictly between groups. They join when mean flatness is at most 0.30, or mean normalized byte entropy is at least 0.62 with mean positive-cell count at least 60, or that entropy is below 0.62 with mean count at least 3.5. Incomplete evidence leaves groups separate and held for review. Text alone does not absorb a varied final tail.

Byte entropy is `-sum(p * ln(p)) / ln(256)` over one histogram of every BGR byte in the loaded thumbnail, before resizing for inference. Flatness uses separate 256-bin B, G and R histograms: choose each channel's first modal bin, sum bins in `[max(0, peak - 3), min(255, peak + 3))`, and average the three fractions outside that band. The upper limit is exclusive: value 255 is always excluded, and a solid white thumbnail scores 1 rather than 0. Gap and tail statistics average per-frame measurements. Every score-map cell at or above the manifest threshold counts (0.999 for the supplied model); positions use the mean column/row indexes divided by the map width/height, without a half-cell offset. Extraction preserves display aspect with `scale=320:320:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase` (569 by 320 for the S05 files). Inference stretches that thumbnail to 320 square with linear interpolation and no padding. Model/runtime, source PTS sampling and JPEG differences still prevent a claim of exact Plex parity.

The reported frame indexes are one-based: subtracting one selects the corresponding array element, rather than an earlier sample. Start and raw end are those chosen frames' source PTS. EOF is a boundary value, never a synthetic evidence frame. The configured playback offsets apply afterward; `ReachesFileEnd` is true only when the supported playback end equals the visual timeline end. Boundaries carry one sampling interval of uncertainty. The earlier fine layout refinement does not override these coarse group placements; layout measurements and their profile thresholds are also used by the sparse-scene safeguard above. Incomplete evidence never extends the end.

The helper must advertise `imageFeatureVersion: "bgr-byte-square-v3"` and the paired manifest must specify `resizePolicy: "Stretch"`. Missing or invalid measurements cannot support these decisions. Helper environment, evidence cache and detector profile identities include the feature and decoder versions; the profile also records group ordering and index convention. Older letterbox measurements cannot stand in for the square input. All results remain `NeedsReview`, including all-rejected and no-group results, and the detector advertises no production approval. Saved human decisions, canonical segments and exports are not automatically rewritten.
